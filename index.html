Section 02 — Emotions

Emotions Shape
Every Decision

Emotions are not obstacles — they are data. Each feeling carries a biochemical signature that colors perception and steers every choice we make.

Happiness
Activates the reward circuits and releases dopamine — the molecule of motivation.
Confidence
Strengthens the prefrontal cortex, enabling clearer thinking and decisive action.
Fear
Triggers the amygdala — a survival signal refined over 500 million years of evolution.
Curiosity
Floods the brain with norepinephrine — the fuel of exploration and discovery.
